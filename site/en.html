Recruits' questions

What is Operation Black Lungs?

An Android app to quit smoking or vaping. A drill sergeant gives you one voiced order a day for 365 days, helps you hold out against cravings and tracks your progress: days held, money saved, trophies.

Why a sergeant who insults you?

Because some people are fed up with apps that congratulate them for nothing. The Sergeant is tough, funny and crude. Adults only.

How much does it cost?

SOS, Cig Crush, the war report, trophies, boot camp, the Arsenal and buddy mode are free, forever. The full program (all 365 orders and the Sergeant's calls) comes with a 7-day free trial, then a one-time purchase of $39.99 (no subscription), or $6.99 a month or $14.99 a quarter, cancel anytime in Google Play. Prices are shown in your currency in the app.

I already quit. Is it still useful?

Yes. When you enlist, pick “I already quit” and the date of your first smoke-free day: those days count as held, and the Sergeant takes it from there.

Does the Sergeant really call me?

Yes, inside the app: at your weakest hours your phone rings and the Sergeant appears on video. It isn't a phone call, and you can turn calls off in your profile.

Does it work for vaping?

Yes. Vape mode adapts the program, the numbers and the orders to vaping.

What about iPhone?

For now, the app is launching on Android only. On iPhone? Vote for the iOS version under the sign-up form: the more votes, the sooner it comes.

Is my data safe?

Everything stays on your phone. Only buddy mode and recruit codes, if you use them, go through our server, and you can delete it all from the app. No ads, no trackers.

Is it a medical treatment?

No. Operation Black Lungs is not a medical device and is not a substitute for medical advice. For support, talk to your doctor or pharmacist.

When does the app launch?

Launch on Google Play is planned for December 4, 2026. Until then, it's in closed testing. Sign your order at the top of the page: you'll get an email on launch day.
